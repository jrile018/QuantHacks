## Contract and invariant

CIK is an issuer, ticker a label, security/instrument ID a dated tradable object. Validate share class, listing/action interval, option deliverable or actual futures contract as applicable. Issuer, market and portfolio scopes differ. One market outcome is not duplicated as multiple issuer opportunities.

Decision identity binds instrument/scope, information cutoff, calendar/session, horizon and experiment mode. Preserve distinct issuer15:30, close-diagnostic16:00 and futures09:30 protocols; dates/timezones alone do not align their information sets.

## Impact and frontier
