# First backtest process

Universe: live partial research pipeline with planned accepted consumer capsule. Status: partial.

## Inputs and dependencies

Versioned protocol/calendar/registry; dated decision/universe/instrument evidence; source/availability records; causal features; dated target/quote records; class-specific costs/risk/lifecycle where economic replay is claimed.

## Process

1. Producer publishes exact immutable manifest and a small real fixture, retaining unqualified/missing/quarantined rows.
2. Consumer validates fingerprints, schema/grain, identity, clocks, units, math and exclusions; checks counterexamples. Record accepted scope and rejected reasons.
3. Post Benchmark composes a capsule with actual command, source/environment, pinned inputs, adapter/protocol, fresh output and gate report.
4. Diagnostic replay verifies routing/coverage. Eligible comparison fits only usable data and preserves opportunity/rejection ledgers.
5. Portfolio backtest proceeds independently per asset only after execution/cost/capital/lifecycle and mandate gates pass. Holdout stays protected.

Post Benchmark reports the thin peer consumer has imported the actual three-row Lattice fixture once for diagnostics. The exact consumer receipt is tracked separately from economic acceptance; the integrated costed capsule remains pending. Existing readiness runners can produce insufficient/zero eligible outputs without fitting.

## First incremental boundary

Lattice actual2024close fixture → thin Post Benchmark diagnostics consumer. Financial facts/industry observations/quotes qualify separately; this fixture cannot bridge to15:30 or turn unknown mapping/receipts into approved labels.

## Failure and recovery

Reject wrong fingerprints or semantics; keep failed/unknown opportunities; do not use zero price/empty source as a valid label. Resume from an accepted immutable checkpoint. No duplicate fitting, source requests or paid submissions.

## Acceptance and human decision

Required final artifact: an exact runnable capsule with actual run evidence and class-specific eligibility table. The user selected a small validated slice with realistic costs, then expansion. A valid eligible comparison with no reliable after-cost improvement may complete with an explicit no-trade result and targeted follow-up research. Missing clocks, quotes or eligible observations remain insufficient and do not establish no edge. Full planned scope remains retained regardless of sequencing.
