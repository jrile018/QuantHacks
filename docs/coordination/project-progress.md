## Intended result

Build an event-driven research system that combines company financial benchmarks, public filings and industry information, then tests whether signals improve a realistically costed portfolio. The first milestone is one frozen **post-release wording signal in equities**. Financial benchmark surprise, prediction markets, regimes, options/futures and GPU acceleration have separate later gates.

The first research account is hypothetical USD1 million: long and short equities, equally sized eligible positions, maximum100% combined gross exposure, no leveraged economic exposure and zero idle-cash yield. Post selects/freezes a feasible existing recipe before scoring. Short borrow, collateral, corporate actions, dividends and costs must be accounted for. These are directly verified human choices in Organize Benchmark Data Push; they supersede long-only proposals. Reporting qualified headlines versus an explicitly provisional scenario remains pending in that chat.
