## Existing evidence

The completed first pass tested generic residual peer relationships and graph changes. Neither numerical packet beat simple forecast controls across the examined equity/futures markets. Graph risk filters worsened downside against the scaled ordinary control; direct-signal evidence was inconclusive. The full options diagnostic retained 18 qualifying events, too sparse for its fixed gate. These observations justify rejecting those specific tested rules; they do not establish that every context-conditioned mechanism is impossible.

## Research distinction

A relationship number measures unusualness/association. The prediction also needs a mechanism explaining what should happen afterward, the economic exposure linking the assets, and proof that the supposed information was available before the decision. A surprising move alone does not determine reversal versus continuation.
