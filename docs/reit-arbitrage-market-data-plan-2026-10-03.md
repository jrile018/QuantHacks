# REIT arbitrage and repricing: market-data plan

**Research status: 2026-10-03.** Desk research only. No market-data purchase or bulk collection was made. The user’s monthly data ceiling is unspecified; every acquisition below therefore has a quote-and-approval gate. This plan covers all validated US-listed REIT issuers from 2024-01-01 onward, including chronological out-of-sample evaluation. It does not assert that the project’s current account has these entitlements.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Maintain two hypotheses. **Strict arbitrage** requires simultaneous executable legs, matched cash flows, sizes, deliverables, fees, financing, dividends, borrow, exercise/assignment, margin and latency. A historical midpoint cannot prove a fill. **Disclosure repricing** forecasts risky returns after a filing becomes usable and is evaluated net of costs out of sample. A futures hedge may reduce portfolio risk, but issuer-versus-index exposure is not arbitrage.

The current options output is not an all-REIT sample: [config.py](../src/config.py) sets `EVENT_TAG = "cfo_appointment"`, uses a static top-100 list, and tests 2024–2025 with 2026 OOS. [Databento options backfill](databento-options-backfill.md) documents 654 CFO-study contracts, not complete REIT issuers or options coverage. Reuse its quote-generation pipeline only after a new dated universe and contract manifest are made.

## Staged acquisition: stop at each gate

1. **Universe and filing spine, no quote purchase.** Build issuer CIK, dated US common-share listings, ticker/security/exchange intervals, first/last trading dates, REIT-type evidence, merger/delisting consideration, filing accession/type, acceptance/publication/usable times, and evidence references. Freeze validated inclusion rules before outcomes. Extract all eligible disclosure families and ordinary-day controls without selecting by future return. This is necessary to say “all validated REITs.”

2. **Broad, inexpensive coverage screen.** Request vendor estimates before any paid request. Use daily raw/adjusted stock bars and actions for every issuer/date; free/basic access where entitled, otherwise quote the subscription. Preserve filings and controls. Check listing dates, REIT subtype, delistings, ticker changes, gaps and actions. Shortlisting must use a preregistered rule based only on point-in-time filing metadata and pre-decision market data; freeze thresholds on development data and apply unchanged to OOS. Never rank/select holdout candidates by their outcomes or post-event data. Daily/minute aggregates are screening marks, not arbitrage evidence; report coverage and exclusion reasons.

3. **Targeted synchronized history for preregistered candidates.** For each event selected by the frozen past-only rule, request same-session stock trades/NBBO and exact options contracts mappable from decision-time data. Map OCC/OPRA symbols, multiplier/deliverable, exercise style, expiry/strike, actions and contract lifecycle. Preserve conditions, venue, bid/ask size, source clocks, session/status and retrieval time. Add open interest only with its availability clock. Use CBBO-1s for screening; use CMBP-1 only to assess quote-update age/persistence and synchronized consolidated BBO. Neither supports per-venue queue reconstruction or fill proof. Compare a sample against Massive historical quotes.

4. **Futures and execution inputs only where relevant.** No issuer-specific futures were verified. For hedge/context analysis, map XAR and RX/JR separately with dated definitions/rules; request trades/settlements, BBO sizes and historical volume/OI, then MBO on a small preregistered sample if liquidity justifies book/queue study. Validate actual hours, expiry, tick, multiplier, rolls, settlement and historical quote availability. A futures/sector relationship is a proxy result. Strict stock/options arbitrage separately needs dated borrow, fees, financing/margin, distributions, exercise/assignment and executable legs; missing historical borrow or fill evidence blocks an executable-arbitrage conclusion.

**Common schema:** event and issuer/security/contract IDs; valid/known-time mappings; source/schema/version/hash and symbology; venue; raw bid/ask/trade, size and conditions; source clocks plus actual local receipt/processing (null if unavailable); action/deliverable, session/calendar and quote-age; cost/borrow/margin inputs; coverage/missing reason. Never call a vendor clock local receipt. Midpoints, sampled BBO and closes are marks; a conservative crossing model is an assumption, not observed fill proof.

## Quote request and budget gate

Before acquisition, provide per-source estimates separating **one-time history and recurring subscriptions**. List product/schema, rights, mapped symbols, dates, sessions/resolution, fields, API or files, record/byte estimate, fees, redownload policy and quote date. Split stocks/options/futures; do not request all OPRA series. Save Databento `metadata.get_cost` inputs/results. For Massive list tier/recurring fee and confirm history/license. For CME request product/date-specific quote. Compare totals to the monthly ceiling before acquisition.

Useful cost anchor, not a REIT estimate: the project’s existing [options backfill ledger](databento-options-backfill.md) reports $4.7966 quoted for 654 selected contracts’ OPRA CBBO request and $11.74521 actual for daily bars over roughly 2.3 years. This CFO sample is tiny relative to all listed REITs and says nothing about a full-chain quote. Do not linearly extrapolate it to the REIT universe or infer remaining account credit.

## Evaluation and readiness

After the coverage audit, split chronologically, group issuers/events, purge overlapping labels, fit only in development, and keep final holdout sealed until protocol freeze. Report full-universe coverage and validated-subset results; paired baselines use identical events/clocks. Measure net fixed-horizon returns, sector/factor controls, uncertainty clustered by issuer/date, trial count, costs, capacity and drawdown. Keep futures hedge P&L separate from portfolio loss reduction. Then run a frozen forward paper stream.

**Readiness:** ready to create a dated REIT universe, event spine, quote manifests and provider cost requests. Not ready for acquisition, full-REIT completeness claims, OOS conclusions, fill claims or strict-arbitrage classification until (a) validated historical issuer/listing universe is frozen, (b) monthly ceiling and data rights are confirmed, (c) exact product quotes/coverage checks pass, and (d) synchronized contract/borrow/cash-flow inputs support the claimed payoff. Existing CFO options outputs do not satisfy these gates.
