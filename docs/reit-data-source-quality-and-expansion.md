## Evidence requirements before financial or trading use

For each agreement or financial metric, preserve issuer/borrower identity, CIK, accession, exact exhibit filename, report/effective period, raw hash, locator and exact quote. Review value, units, scale, currency, accounting basis and dimensions explicitly. An agreement amount is not automatically a drawn balance, lender exposure or observed cash movement. CompanyFacts accession/units/period context must agree with the filed source; absence of a tag is not a zero value.

Keep reported date, filing date, SEC acceptance time, retrieval time, memo posted/effective date and observed public availability separate. A current retrieval proves availability at that observation, not the first public time. Neither an acceptance timestamp nor a later receipt alone establishes executable information time for a backtest.

The next cheap acquisition priorities are contract-specific OCC memos for actual affected series, all study-window exchange calendars, and SEC exhibit documents selected from known filing indexes. Treasury/FRED rates add context only. Expanding historical membership needs dated issuer inclusion/exclusion, REIT qualification, listings, mergers and delistings with cross-source reconciliation; current tables and a handful of monthly PDFs cannot certify full history. No paid requests, messages, OCR audit or full financial-accuracy audit are part of this catalog expansion.
