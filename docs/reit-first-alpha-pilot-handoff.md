## Minimum concrete packet for Post Benchmark

| Layer | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Wording evidence | Existing frozen fragment scores plus one newly registered aggregate/rule; exact text/version, source/accession/raw and derived hashes, eligible quoted spans, provider/definition pins and missing/truncated outputs |
| Information time | A verified conservative public upper bound for that exact text/version plus declared historical processing latency can support a delayed replay; retain earliest/actual public, received and processed clocks as unknown where unobserved. SEC acceptance alone is insufficient |
| Security identity | Dated issuer/CIK, common-share class, ticker/venue/currency and vendor instrument mappings at both endpoints; verified price and quantity units |
| Lifecycle/actions | Dated split, dividend/distribution, merger/delisting and deliverable coverage over the selected window, with rights and adjustment convention |
| Quotes | Bid/ask and sizes at canonical entry/exit; exact timestamps, source hashes and proven freshness/availability or an accepted historical replay assumption; no minute endpoint treated as a quote update |
| Economics | Explicit quantity and funded cash, commissions/fees, quoted spread charged once, incremental slippage, financing and any borrow/margin/lifecycle obligations that apply |
| Protocol | One wording signal, matched baseline and cash, fixed daily/next-session clock, development opportunities and exclusions, trial registry, success/stop/inconclusive criteria, and protected final-test policy |

## Decisions after the pilot

- **Expand:** a complete, matched, costed development comparison supports the predeclared incremental information mechanism and acceptable risk; confirm through the protected evaluation sequence before adding layers.
- **Stop this candidate:** a complete comparison shows no useful incremental benefit after the specified costs. Preserve the result and trial history.
- **Inconclusive:** too few independent disclosures, uncertain clocks, missing quotes/actions/costs or unstable estimates prevent a conclusion. Missing data does not establish no alpha.

A null result can challenge this signal, cohort, timing and implementation. It cannot falsify all loan-network information, all REITs, strict arbitrage, options or futures. Previously inspected 2024–2025 data remains development. Verify contamination and freeze the strategy before selecting or opening final OOS; use prospective sealed evaluation if no historical period meets that rule.
