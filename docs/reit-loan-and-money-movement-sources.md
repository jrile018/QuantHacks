# REIT loan and money movement document research

Researched **2026-10-03**. This is a source map for collecting *reported* financing, loan, and cash movement from public REIT documents. It uses this repository's company data as the starting universe; it does not claim that all of those issuers have cached options contracts or that public filings reveal each bank transfer.

## 1. Which companies from this project?

The configured options study in `src/config.py` has 100 underlying tickers. Joining those tickers to `industries.csv` on ticker and selecting SIC **6798** yields **AMT (American Tower)** as the sole REIT-classified constituent. The saved `data/processed/options_expiration_inventory.csv` has **744 GD contracts and no REIT contracts**. Thus AMT is the direct options-universe pilot; a REIT-wide study requires a separately defined universe and options-data retrieval.

For expansion, `industries.csv` has **174 ticker rows** labeled SIC 6798, and the local `data/processed/tiger_8k_company_sectors.csv` has **53 CIK rows** labeled SIC 6798 among **1,630 CIKs**. The existing [53-issuer source map](reit-source-map.md) links every one of those CIKs to its SEC filings. These are **screening counts**, not 174 distinct confirmed active REIT common stocks: rows can be preferred shares, notes, stale tickers, or misclassified entities. For example, some mortgage REITs carry a broader real-estate SIC, while some SIC 6798 rows may not be useful public equity targets. Validate each issuer's present identity, REIT status, security type, and CIK against its SEC filings and the [Nareit directory](https://www.reit.com/investing/reit-directory) before ingestion. The local 8-K export is an event sample, not a complete REIT universe.

**Useful first cohorts from the local files:**

The equity/mortgage distinction matters: a mortgage REIT's **loans receivable** are assets, while an equity REIT's **mortgage notes payable** are liabilities. A REIT can have both. Use issuer CIK as the stable key and a dated ticker history as an attribute.

## 2. Best websites and documents, ranked by collection value

| Priority | Website / entry point | Documents and formats | What can be collected | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [SEC EDGAR company search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/) and the [Submissions API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, 424B, exhibits; HTML/iXBRL, XML, occasional PDF | Dated filing manifest, cash-flow statements, debt notes, liquidity discussion, major financing transactions and contracts | Filing text must be read with its as-of period and amendments; ticker may change |
| 2 | Each REIT's investor-relations financials and quarterly results pages | Earnings supplements, investor decks, annual reports; PDF and sometimes XLS/XLSX | Debt maturity schedules, debt instrument detail, loan portfolio roll-forwards, originations/repayments, occupancy, property acquisitions/dispositions, dividend history | Often unaudited/non-GAAP; reconcile material amounts to 10-Q/K and retain the release date |
| 3 | [SEC Financial Statement and Notes Data Sets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-notes-data-sets) | Bulk ZIP: NUM/TXT and related XBRL tables | Scalable tagged facts, including notes and custom/dimensional disclosures that the simpler API omits | As-filed flat data, large downloads, context/rounding issues; source filing remains authoritative |
| 4 | [SEC Company Facts API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) | Per-CIK JSON and bulk `companyfacts.zip` | Standard, entity-wide cash-flow, balance-sheet and debt facts across periods | Does **not** include custom tags or many dimensional/property/loan facts |
| 5 | SEC filings of **securitization trusts**, reached through [EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/) | 10-D distribution reports, ABS-EE asset files, prospectuses and servicing exhibits | Where applicable, collateral and payment/distribution information below the REIT level | Trust CIK differs from REIT CIK; only relevant deals/assets; do not attribute whole trust cash flows to the REIT without ownership/servicing evidence |
| 6 | [Nareit directory](https://www.reit.com/investing/reit-directory) | Sector/company pages | Classify current REIT sector and discover issuer IR sites | Discovery layer, not a cash ledger or source of a company's debt contract |
| 7 | Jurisdictional property recorders, e.g. [NYC ACRIS](https://www.nyc.gov/site/finance/taxes/acris.page) | Recorded mortgages, satisfactions, deeds, UCC documents and images | Verify a specific property's liens, parties and recording dates | Entity subsidiaries and property identifiers must be mapped; recorded face amounts are not proof of cash drawn or repaid; access varies by county |

The SEC documents explain the APIs' no-key access and their standard-taxonomy/entity-wide scope; the notes datasets include more detailed as-filed data and explicitly say they are not a substitute for reading the filing. [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) · [SEC notes datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-notes-data-sets)

### Which form answers which question?

| Question | Start with | Then confirm in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How much cash came in and went out this period? | 10-Q/10-K **statement of cash flows**: operating, investing and financing sections | Notes and MD&A for gross-versus-net detail; compare exact reporting periods |
| How much debt is outstanding, at what rate, and when due? | 10-Q/10-K debt/credit-facility notes; issuer supplemental debt schedules | Credit agreement, indenture, prospectus and subsequent 8-K amendments |
| Was new debt actually raised or repaid? | Financing cash flows plus contemporaneous 8-K/424B and debt-note roll-forward | Settlement/closing announcement and next filed quarter; facility size alone is not a draw |
| Which property was bought or sold? | 8-K transaction exhibits, 10-Q/K acquisition/disposition note, supplemental | Property schedules and local recorder where relevant |
| Which loans did a mortgage REIT originate or receive repayment on? | 10-Q/K loan portfolio roll-forward, issuer supplement and earnings exhibit | Schedule IV where filed, loan-level note, and deal/trust filings where applicable |
| What cash was distributed to shareholders? | Financing cash flows, dividend 8-K/releases and equity note | Payment date and share class; distinguish declaration from payment |

In a 10-K, also look for **Schedule III—Real Estate and Accumulated Depreciation** for property/encumbrance context and **Schedule IV—Mortgage Loans on Real Estate** where filed for loan descriptions, rates, maturity, face/carrying amount and delinquency. Availability and granularity vary. [Realty Income Schedule III example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/726728/000072672825000055/o-20241231.htm) · [BXMT Schedule IV example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1061630/000106163026000009/R33.htm)

## 4. Research/collection sequence

1. **Define entities.** Start with AMT from the configured options 100. For a broader REIT study, seed tickers from SIC 6798 in `industries.csv`, merge the 53 SIC 6798 CIKs from the 8-K export, and add REITs with broader SICs only after issuer/Nareit verification. Record common/preferred/debt security class separately.
2. **Build the filing manifest by CIK.** Use `https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK##########.json`, including older `filings.files`, to find the exact accession, filing date, form and primary document. Use the accession index to enumerate exhibits. Keep 10-K/A and 10-Q/A as separate versions. [SEC API specification](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)
3. **Extract the reported flow first.** From 10-K/Q, collect dated cash-flow lines: operating cash, acquisitions/property purchases, capital expenditure, debt proceeds, debt repayments, equity issuance/repurchases and dividends. Store unit/currency, period start/end, sign and filing URL. Do not infer individual bank transfers from quarterly totals.
4. **Add debt/loan position detail.** Parse debt tables, commitments, effective rates, collateral, covenants and maturity schedules. For mREITs, separately parse loans **receivable**, originations, repayments, sales, modifications, nonaccruals, allowances and funding liabilities. Retain the original table and any management-defined measures.
5. **Connect event documents.** For 8-K Items 1.01/2.03 and exhibits EX-10/EX-4, link the agreement to the next cash-flow/debt position update. For 424B, capture offering terms and net proceeds. For issuer EX-99 or supplements, collect reported transaction dates and reconcile them to periodic reports. The [AAT filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1500217/0001500217-26-000012-index.htm) shows the 8-K → EX-10.1 path in one place.
6. **Use deal/property records only when justified.** If a loan is securitized, identify the trust and examine its own 10-D/ABS-EE documents. For named property mortgages, search the recorder in the correct jurisdiction. Match borrower/OP/subsidiary names and collateral before assigning a record to a REIT.

Suggested event row: `issuer_cik, issuer_ticker_asof, issuer_or_subsidiary, event_type, announced_date, effective_date, cash_date_if_disclosed, amount, currency, amount_kind (commitment|draw|proceeds|repayment|balance|noncash), loan_asset_or_debt_liability, counterparty_if_disclosed, collateral_or_property, form, accession, exhibit_url, reporting_period, evidence_quote_or_table, confidence, reconciliation_status`.

**Important distinctions:** A line of credit commitment is borrowing **capacity**, not cash movement. Loan principal, interest, reserve release, asset sale proceeds and dividends are different kinds of flow. REIT FFO/AFFO and distributable earnings are performance measures, not GAAP cash flow or a bank ledger; source and preserve each issuer's definition. [SEC-hosted REIT FFO disclosure example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/895417/000089541724000014/supplementalinformationtof.htm)

## 5. Practical access and quality boundaries

- SEC public API access requires no API key. Automated use should declare a contact-bearing `User-Agent`, cache requests and observe the SEC's **10 requests/second** limit across workers. The existing `src/config.py` contact string is a placeholder; replace it with an actual team contact before scripted access. [SEC access policy](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data)
- HTML/iXBRL and XML usually preserve more structure than a PDF rendering. Extract those first. Use PDF text extraction for born-digital investor supplements and OCR only for scanned pages.
- Company Facts gives entity-wide standard tags; issuer-specific loan schedules and property detail often require original filing tables, the richer notes dataset, or investor supplements. [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) · [SEC notes datasets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-notes-data-sets)
- Public filings disclose material/aggregated activity and selected loans; they do **not** provide a complete transaction-level cash ledger or every borrower payment. If the research goal is actual account-by-account money movement, public documents alone cannot deliver that coverage.

**Recommended pilot:** collect AMT's last four 10-Q/K filings plus material financing 8-Ks, then contrast one property REIT (AAT or EQR), one commercial lender (BXMT or ABR), and one agency mREIT (AGNC). This tests four distinct disclosure patterns before broadening the ticker universe and paying the cost of document parsing.
