REIT DOCUMENT URL EXPLANATIONS
Researched 2026-10-03. Numbers match the lines in reit_document_urls.txt.

1. SEC EDGAR company search. Search by company name, ticker, or CIK to find a REIT's filing history. Filter for 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and prospectuses. Starting point, not a single financial statement.

2. SEC EDGAR API documentation. Explains the per-CIK Submissions and Company Facts JSON endpoints and bulk archives. Use these to build a filing manifest and pull standardized financial facts at scale; source filings still need review.

3. SEC Financial Statement and Notes Data Sets. Downloadable ZIP files with detailed tagged financial statements and notes, including custom and dimensional facts. Useful for debt notes and large-scale extraction; large files and as-filed data require careful validation.

4. Nareit REIT directory. Use to discover companies/sectors and reach issuer websites. This current member directory includes international firms and other public real-estate companies; validate REIT status and dated US listing separately. It is not a complete historical list of all US listed REITs or evidence of a particular loan.

5. American Tower (AMT) Q2 2026 Form 10-Q. Direct SEC filing with cash-flow statements, liquidity, outstanding debt, borrowings, repayments, and capital spending. AMT is the REIT-classified name in the repository's configured 100-ticker options universe.

6. American Tower September 2026 Form 8-K. Reports a completed senior-notes offering and intended uses of the proceeds. Use it to identify a financing event; confirm later repayments in subsequent filings rather than treating stated intentions as paid cash.

7. American Assets Trust (AAT) April 2026 8-K filing index. Lists the filed 8-K and attached credit agreement (EX-10.1). The index is useful for identifying the exact source documents and their filing date.

8. American Assets Trust credit agreement, Exhibit 10.1. The full agreement identifies borrower, guarantor, lenders, facility terms, interest, prepayment, maturity, and covenants. A stated credit limit is borrowing capacity, not the amount actually drawn.

9. Blackstone Mortgage Trust (BXMT) Q2 2026 Form 10-Q. Direct SEC filing for a commercial mortgage REIT. Look for mortgage loans receivable, credit/risk ratings, loan activity, funding liabilities, cash flow, and allowances.

10. Blackstone Mortgage Trust Q2 2026 investor exhibit. Filed presentation discussing investments, loan originations, repayments, portfolio composition, and financing. Match its management-defined activity metrics to the 10-Q before combining values.

11. Arbor Realty Trust (ABR) 2025 Form 10-K. Audited annual SEC report with statements of cash flows, loan/investment portfolio, credit losses, debt, securitizations, and notes. A primary source for commercial mortgage lending activity.

12. Arbor Realty Trust filed annual-report PDF. PDF version of the annual report for reading or PDF extraction. Useful for tables and loan schedules; link extracted figures back to the related SEC 10-K and exact pages.

13. Equity Residential (EQR) Q2 2026 earnings release and supplemental information. Issuer webpage with debt summary, debt maturity schedule, covenants, rental/property measures, and capital expenditures. It is supplemental/unaudited, so compare key numbers with the 10-Q.

14. Digital Realty (DLR) Q2 2026 earnings supplement PDF. Direct PDF with debt maturities, balance sheet, capital expenditures, acquisitions/dispositions, and real-estate operating tables. Useful for property REIT financing and investment flows.

15. AGNC Investment (AGNC) Q2 2026 stockholder presentation PDF. Officially linked presentation for an agency mortgage REIT. Focus on mortgage-backed securities, repurchase-agreement financing, leverage, and hedges; its model differs from a whole-loan commercial lender. Validation on 2026-10-03 confirmed the official issuer link, but access to the PDF returned 403/tool errors; the content remains unverified in this live check.

Public filings report material or aggregated financial activity. They do not provide a complete transaction-level bank ledger. Preserve filing date, reporting period, issuer CIK, currency, and document/page when recording any amount.

See reit_source_validation_2026-10-03.txt for the dated source checks. Cached-file integrity and current live URL availability are separate checks; accessible discovery pages do not establish whole-universe coverage.
