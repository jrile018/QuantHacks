## Integration contract, not another competing pipeline

Use the [strategy-validation spine](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-strategy-validation.md): dated decisions/features/labels, quote feasibility, common trial registry, and shared cash/positions ledger. The existing parallel plan remains the work schedule; the validation plan is the canonical proposed typed-record/evaluation contract. Proposed new names are extensions/adapters, not duplicate collectors or competing schema definitions.

Stocks need actual observed prices and corporate actions; synthetic stock marks from options parity are diagnostics. Futures need raw contract definitions, calendars, causal rolls and cash/margin accounting. Options retain their own selection/deliverable/quote rules. A shared timestamp does not guarantee synchronous execution; retain source semantics, age, closed-market states and uncertainty for each instrument.

Issuer decisions and market/portfolio decisions have distinct scope. Futures context may join many company rows, but a shared ES outcome cannot become hundreds of independent observations or positions. Use one unique market-instrument decision label, aggregate company signals under a frozen policy and net positions in one account ledger.

Loan-network edges retain borrower/lender/guarantor/collateral/ownership relation, stated amount kind, source evidence, publication/known time, economic validity and confidence. A company loan is different from the securities borrow required to short its stock. Do not equate revolver capacity with drawn cash, aggregate debt with an identified loan, or a missing disclosure with absence of an economic connection. Include subsidiaries and private counterparties without fabricating tradable ticker mappings.

From January 2024 onward, reserve chronological evaluation using mature labels and documented holdout access. Earlier documents and market lookbacks can support initial features without becoming outcome rows. Future graph edges, later universe classifications, final same-day bars or back-adjusted rolls cannot rewrite earlier information. Current inspection of old data prevents calling it untouched automatically.
