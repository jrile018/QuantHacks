## Feed choices and historical coverage

**Official provider facts:** Massive advertises listed-US stock bars, trades and NBBO quotes from 2003-09-10; the accessible window depends on subscription. Its stock quote schema distinguishes bid/ask prices, sizes, venue identifiers, participant and SIP timestamps. SIP receipt is not the research system's receipt. January 2024 is within advertised history, but symbol/date/endpoint entitlement and gaps require audit. [Historical coverage](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-much-historical-stock-data-does-massive-have), [stock quotes](https://www.massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/trades-quotes/quotes).

Consolidated trades and SIP NBBO, venue BBO, and direct-feed reconstructed BBO have different evidence contracts. Nasdaq Basic supplies Nasdaq-system trading information for securities listed elsewhere; broad symbol coverage does not make it consolidated NBBO. NYSE Daily TAQ explicitly supplies consolidated trades, quotes and NBBO files. Trades describe prints; quotes describe displayed prices and liquidity. Neither guarantees the research account's fill. Preserve conditions, corrections, session and lot/size conventions. [Nasdaq Basic](https://www.nasdaq.com/solutions/data/equities/nasdaq-basic/factsheet), [NYSE TAQ specification](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/nyse/data/Daily_TAQ_Client_Spec_v1.6.pdf).

Databento's current `EQUS.MINI` is a derived, component-venue aggregated BBO with quantities including odd lots; trade venue identity is anonymized. Treat it as its own feed, with coverage and routing assumptions. `XNAS.ITCH` advertises history from 2018-05-01, with schema-specific variation, and represents Nasdaq venue activity. `EQUS.SUMMARY` provides final daily OHLCV from the approximately 20:15 ET summary, including post-market volume; that day's final summary is unavailable at an earlier decision. [Mini specification](https://databento.com/docs/venues-and-datasets/equs-mini), [Nasdaq history](https://databento.com/datasets/XNAS.ITCH), [Summary specification](https://databento.com/docs/venues-and-datasets/equs-summary).

The [historical API reference](https://databento.com/docs/api-reference-historical?historical=http) lists `EQUS.SIP`; its dedicated public specification and historical start were not verified here. Proposed provider selection must capture available datasets, exact schema ranges, condition/gap metadata and entitlements before assuming January 2024 SIP coverage. Reuse compatible caches first. Paid-dataset planning is requested: compare rights, history, revisions, feed semantics and cost for a bounded pilot and expansion through the shared acquisition ledger. The monthly spending limit will follow; do not infer it from the old options budget or initiate purchases. No cohort cost or entitlement follows from options access.

## Identity, adjustments and accounting

Create stable issuer-to-security-to-listing links with dated ticker/share-class/currency/type/exchange intervals. CIK identifies an issuer, not one uniquely tradable security. Include renamed, acquired and delisted securities, listing starts and terminal consideration; ticker reuse must never splice unrelated companies. Sector/REIT classifications and company relationships need evidence, valid time, known time and revision history. Provider historical ticker queries help reconstruct associations but require completeness checks. [Massive historical ticker design](https://www.ny5.massive.com/blog/new-point-in-time-tickers-api).

Massive aggregate bars default to split adjustment, with raw bars available through `adjusted=false`; trades/quotes remain raw and aggregates are not dividend-adjusted. Preserve raw observations for execution and cash accounting. Build separately versioned split-consistent price-return and dividend-inclusive total-return benchmarks. Future splits used to restate historical nominal prices cannot enter decision-time price thresholds or capitalization. [Adjustment semantics](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends).

Maintain share quantities across splits, cash and distributed securities across dividends/spinoffs/mergers, dividend receivables and payment dates, financing, collateral, borrow and fees. Total-return conventions must state reinvestment; actual account cash follows payment timing. Never add dividends again to an already dividend-inclusive return. Massive distinguishes actual `cash_amount` from today's-share-basis `split_adjusted_cash_amount`. Databento offers revision history through corporate actions `pit=True`; default reduction retains the latest record. Its `ts_record` is vendor record-change time, requiring availability interpretation rather than invented historical local receipt. [Dividend fields](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/does-massive-adjust-historic-dividends-for-splits), [PIT actions](https://databento.com/docs/venues-and-datasets/corporate-actions), [timestamp schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/corporate-actions).

Optional short legs require dated borrow availability, indicative versus realized rates, recall/buy-in treatment and distribution obligations. Today's shortable list cannot backfill historical locates; an indicative rate does not guarantee borrow. Unknown borrow blocks an executable-short claim while permitting an explicitly hypothetical sensitivity study. [Broker availability](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/trading/short-securities-availability.php?menu=A), [short distribution liability](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/campus/glossary-terms/payment-in-lieu-of-dividends/).

## Shared snapshot proposal

Extend the integration owner's versioned market contract rather than creating separate decision clocks. One long-form snapshot row per instrument/anchor preserves:

| Group | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Join | `decision_id`, `snapshot_id`, issuer/security/listing/contract IDs, asset class, dated mapping version, anchor role |
| Source | Provider, dataset, publisher/venue, schema/version, raw symbol, source hash, sequence/correction reference, entitlement/coverage basis |
| Clocks | Original event/participant/SIP/vendor-capture timestamps as supplied, observed local receipt/processing, retrieval, timestamp basis, interval bounds, assumed replay availability/latency separately |
| Market | Raw bid/ask and sizes/units, trade price/size/conditions, original quote age, trading status/session, calendar version, currency, price scale, quality/missing reason |
| Economics | Corporate-action version, adjustment basis, shares/deliverable/multiplier, declared distribution evidence, financing/borrow scenario IDs |

Derived company momentum, realized volatility, spreads, volume, sector/market returns and exposure features reference eligible snapshot IDs and formula versions. Add borrower/lender and peer features only through the dated relationship registry. Keep future stock outcomes in a separate label artifact; labels never populate anticipation snapshots. A stale underlying cannot be made synchronous by copying the option timestamp.

## Validation, ownership and gates

Strict arbitrage needs simultaneously actionable legs and matched enforceable cash flows after exercise, dividends, borrow, financing, spread and costs. Predicted stock repricing bears forecast and market risk. American equity options permit early exercise/assignment; frictionless European parity cannot establish executable US equity-option arbitrage. Treat parity residuals as diagnostics until the full contract/cash-flow/execution audit passes. [OIC parity](https://www.optionseducation.org/advancedconcepts/put-call-parity), [exercise mechanics](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/exercising-options).

Propose an initial coverage audit of six validated REITs across both exposure types, two non-REIT counterparties, and prespecified sector/market references, using ordinary, release and action/identity-boundary sessions where available. Sample early-2024 and later development dates without payoff selection. This is an engineering audit budget, not statistical adequacy or a reduced final universe. Expansion tracks every validated REIT in deterministic batches and reports missing/failed cases.

The equities worker would independently own `src/equities_quote_adapters.py`, `scripts/audit_equities_coverage.py` and equities audit outputs. The identity/relationship owner supplies mappings; the corporate-action owner supplies dated action records; options/futures owners supply their instrument schemas. Integration alone changes shared contracts, experiment configuration and learner joins. OCR does not block market coverage work.

Promotion gates: reviewed mappings and raw/adjusted reconciliation; quote clock/session/gap audit; bounded synchronized entry/exit examples with no fabricated fill or borrow; then frozen paired stock/option comparisons on identical decisions. Fit beta/sector controls and preprocessing within development. Purge overlapping outcomes and group issuer/event/common shocks across splits. Guard against future sector constituents, relationship revisions, final daily volume, corrected future facts and holdout-driven batch selection. Report covered-subset gains plus full-universe exclusions/fallbacks. Unknown delisting payoffs remain unresolved, never zero. Only useful chronological held-out improvement with stated uncertainty, costs and risk limits advances to frozen forward paper evaluation.
