A separate annual-report image is OCR calibration, not an 8-K outcome. ## Public release, filing and collection are different clocks

Form 8-K General Instruction B ordinarily requires filing or furnishing within four business days after a reportable event, with item-specific exceptions. Instructions also permit incorporation of an already published release. Item 2.02/7.01 information and related exhibits are generally furnished unless designated otherwise. Consequently the legal deadline cannot identify the first public announcement or a tradable surprise. [SEC Form 8-K, Instructions B/F](https://www.sec.gov/divisions/corpfin/forms/8-k.htm), accessed 2026-10-03.

Item 2.02’s complementary-presentation exception has a literal 48-hour condition; it is not an earnings forecast clock. An earnings release attached later as EX-99.1 may already have appeared on issuer IR or a newswire. Record each candidate publication with exact timestamp, timezone, URL, preserved bytes and evidence; select the earliest verified substantive release. Date-only or conflicting evidence warrants timing uncertainty or exclusion from intraday claims. [SEC 8-K interpretations, 206.01](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/compliance-disclosure-interpretations/exchange-act-form-8-k), accessed 2026-10-03.

EDGAR acceptance is submission metadata, not proof the market first saw the underlying information then. Some late submissions disseminate the next business day. Preserve raw acceptance strings and verify timezone conversion against filing headers/indexes; flag disagreements. Accession prefixes can identify a filing agent, so use issuer metadata for CIK identity. Retain corrections, deletions and original versions. [SEC accessing EDGAR data](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data), accessed 2026-10-03.

Use submissions `recent` plus referenced historical files; do not assume recent rows cover all history. APIs require no key, update during dissemination, and have nightly bulk archives. SEC describes typical processing delays, not guaranteed delivery times. Companyfacts aggregates standard-taxonomy entity facts across filings: join each fact to its accession and availability; period-end dates cannot certify when figures were knowable. Later restatements must not replace earlier versions in historical features. [SEC public data APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), accessed 2026-10-03.

Future acquisition should cache immutable responses and hashes, use a real contact in User-Agent, and share one request budget across machines below the SEC’s ten-per-second ceiling. The existing catalog defaults to five. Store contact metadata privately; do not log authentication values or full authenticated URLs. [SEC developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources), accessed 2026-10-03. Existing code reads credential names `MASSIVE_API_KEY` and `SEC_CONTACT_EMAIL`; values were not inspected.

## Provider coverage and the replay boundary

Massive `/v3/quotes/{optionsTicker}` supplies historical bid/ask, sizes, exchange identifiers, sequence numbers and nanosecond SIP receipt timestamps. Official coverage begins 2022-03-07; entitlement depends on plan. A successful request proves only that contract/date request. Preserve `next_url`, request ID, pagination caps and truncation separately from genuine no-data responses. The collector currently retains exact SIP nanoseconds and today's retrieval timestamp, correctly leaving historical local receipt null. [Massive options quotes](https://www.massive.com/docs/rest/options/trades-quotes/quotes), accessed 2026-10-03.

Keep source exchange/SIP time, vendor capture time, actual local receipt, local processing completion and retrieval time separate. A historical replay may assume transport and processing latency, but must label each assumed field and test plausible latency sensitivity. Today's document/OCR processing cannot be backdated into observed availability. A live shadow collector is needed to measure the actual end-to-end distribution.

Databento OPRA `ts_event` denotes consolidator processing and tick `ts_recv` denotes vendor capture receipt. Historical instrument IDs can change; use dated raw-symbol mappings. Data before 2023-03-28 is subsampled, lacks some tick schemas and has unsuitable receive-time semantics. Prefer a 2024–2025 discovery window to avoid that particular discontinuity. [Databento OPRA specification](https://databento.com/docs/venues-and-datasets/opra-pillar), accessed 2026-10-03.

For `cbbo-1m`, `ts_recv` is the interval-end boundary and `ts_event` is last-trade time. Quotes may be carried within intervals when only a trade updates, and empty intervals need not print. Thus minute/daily CBBO cannot certify original quote freshness by mapping these fields to the strict importer's tick timestamps. Acquire CMBP-1/tick evidence or specify a distinct sampled-CBBO experiment with appropriate availability/staleness rules. [Databento BBO/CBBO schema](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/bbo), accessed 2026-10-03.

Build issuer/security/ticker validity intervals for mergers, symbol changes, delistings and multiple share classes. Retrieve contract reference data explicitly `as_of`, including multiplier, exercise style and additional deliverables; the default is today. [Massive contract overview](https://www.massive.com/docs/rest/options/contracts/contract-overview), accessed 2026-10-03. Review OCC adjustment memos and updates before mapping cash/share deliverables. [OCC information memos](https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemo/search-memo), accessed 2026-10-03.

Use versioned product/date-specific options calendars, holidays and early closes. Contemporary extended-session rules cannot be applied to older years. [Cboe options hours](https://www.cboe.com/about/hours/us-options/), accessed 2026-10-03. Audit zero sizes, crossed/locked markets, age, spreads, gaps, corrections and depth conventions. Last-trade OHLC and trade volume do not supply executable bid/ask or quote depth. Fees, slippage and fills remain assumptions until observed.

## Anticipation cohort and controls

Start discovery with the declared 100-name static cohort over 2024–2025, retaining its selection-date caveat. First inventory dated mappings, session coverage and independent release evidence for every issuer; publish counts at every eligibility gate rather than extrapolating the pilot. A scheduled-earnings subgroup is supportable only where an announcement schedule was public and received before the decision. Forecast schedule updates as successive versions. Without that evidence, use a fixed daily decision grid for every eligible issuer/session, such as a predeclared time before the regular close.

Every decision row exists independently of future filing occurrence. Outcomes may later link to zero, one or multiple releases/accessions in a frozen horizon. Include ordinary days, announced-but-postponed earnings, cancellations and unknown status. Distinguish “no observed event in a fully monitored interval” from right censoring, mapping loss or source outage. An all-8-K event hazard task differs from earnings anticipation: many reportable events are unscheduled, repeat earlier announcements or have different clocks. Do not claim scheduled earnings generalize to all filings.

Current `_event_features` in `src/options_learning.py` requires nonempty `target_accession` for anticipation, although the design document asks for no-event rows. This schema cannot represent genuine controls without fabricated target IDs. Plan a versioned decision-grid interface with nullable outcome links and an independently maintained forbidden future-document set; future accession linkage belongs in labels and split grouping, never the trigger or feature payload. Protect against leakage across exhibits, amendments and duplicate releases, not just one exact accession.

The existing frozen option rule selects a shared expiry 90–180 days away near 120, call near 105% spot and put near 95%, then freezes IDs. Its next-session close targets and separate midpoint/net-return measures can be preserved for this baseline. Audit decision coverage, first executable entry, same-contract exit and mapping validity independently. Group amendments and related releases, purge overlapping label intervals and retain the heldout ledger. A control’s absence of a filing does not imply a zero market return.

## Output interfaces and parallel tasks

Emit `universe_inventory` with cohort, CIK/security, ticker validity, mapping source/version and coverage reason. `decision_grid` contains immutable decision ID/time, issuer/security, trigger type, schedule source/version and censoring status. `release_inventory` contains document/accession/group IDs, item/exhibit role, raw acceptance, verified public/dissemination times, evidence and hashes. `outcome_links` stores nullable accession lists, future release horizon and label availability. Keep predictors in the existing long-form registry with independent lineage.

Quote output preserves `sip_timestamp_ns`/`ts_event_ns`, vendor `ts_recv_ns` where meaningful, actual receipt/processing/retrieval, timestamp basis and flags. Normalized snapshots add anchor, constituent IDs, underlying timestamps, stable contract terms and mapping evidence. Never manufacture synchronization. `coverage_audit` counts eligible, excluded and censored rows by issuer/year/reason. `replay_provenance` versions latency, receipt basis, cost, session and experiment assumptions.

- [ ] **Timing and discovery lane:** bounded catalogs and release/schedule verification produce inventories and uncertainty counts; independent of OCR. Acceptance gate: complete decision denominator and reviewed timezone examples.
- [ ] **Mapping/market lane:** inspect entitlement and sampled dates, produce dated contracts/calendars and small matched decision/entry/exit sets; independent of OCR. Acceptance gate: no tick/interval mislabeling, unreported truncation or fabricated local receipt.
- [ ] **Prior-information lane:** accession-dated fundamentals, prior filings and lagged market features produce a frozen numeric registry; independent of target OCR. Acceptance gate: every source available by decision and no future document family leakage.
- [ ] **Post-release wording lane:** native-text extraction, OCR where needed, independently reviewed references and publication-time evidence produce eligible document features. Actual released wording is available only after its publication, receipt and processing; it cannot enter anticipation.
- [ ] **Integration lane:** after inventories pass, adapt control schemas, freeze split/provenance and run audit-only joins. Later compare prior-information baseline against added wording on identical post-release rows, including market-only and no-trade benchmarks. Sample thresholds in the importer are software gates, not demonstrated statistical power.

Before any purchase, estimate a capped pilot using provider cost/record-count metadata for a few issuer/date intervals spanning normal, wide-spread and release sessions. Compare reusable cached records with the strict timestamp requirements, then estimate expansion from measured records/bytes and current tariff. Maintain one shared acquisition ledger including committed/pending requests, deduplication hashes and cumulative authorized budget; do not treat another thread's documented authorization as a new purchase instruction. No price or cohort-wide cost is inferred from the one-minute sample.

Future large scans, backfills and fitting belong on remote compute under detached tmux; none ran here. Critical decisions are portfolio protection versus standalone call/put selection; static cohort versus historical universe; scheduled earnings versus all-event hazard; decision/holding horizon; strict ticks versus sampled CBBO; and tolerated replay latency/budget. Genuine portfolio protection additionally needs inventory, exposure benchmark, hedge budget and downside-risk metric. Filing rules describe disclosure obligations, not actionable investment recommendations.
