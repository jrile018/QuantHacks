## Contract facts and operational calendars

A future specifies an underlying/reference, expiry, price unit and multiplier, plus settlement/delivery obligations. Margin is collateral, not its contract value. Candidate outright terms are:

| Product | Price/value convention | Minimum outright tick | Terminal obligation |
|---|---|---|---|
| ES | S&P 500 index points; $50/point, value $50 × price | 0.25 point = $12.50 | Cash settlement to special opening quotation |
| MES | S&P 500 index points; $5/point, value $5 × price | 0.25 point = $1.25 | Cash settlement to special opening quotation |
| NQ | Nasdaq-100 index points; $20/point, value $20 × price | 0.25 point = $5 | Cash settlement to special opening quotation |
| ZN | $100,000 face value; points/fractions, $1,000/point | 1/64 point = $15.625 | Deliverable Treasury-note basket |
| SR3 | IMM index = 100 minus compounded SOFR; $2,500/point | 0.005 = $12.50; eligible near expiries 0.0025 = $6.25 | Cash settlement after reference quarter |

These are current specifications; dated rules and definitions govern historical records. ES/MES/NQ scheduled final settlement ordinarily uses the third Friday opening quotation; this differs from a daily settlement or last trade. ZN's deliverable grade is 6½–under-8-year remaining maturity, despite its 10-year product name. Conversion factors, cheapest-to-deliver selection, accrued interest and delivery options affect Treasury basis and sensitivity. [ES rules](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/358/358.pdf), [MES rules](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/353/353.pdf), [NQ rules](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CME/IV/350/359/359.pdf), [ZN rules](https://www.cmegroup.com/rulebook/CBOT/II/19.pdf).

SR3 requires separate named month, reference-quarter start/end, last trade and settlement fields: its named month starts the reference period, while settlement follows approximately three months later. The exchange rule's delivery month denotes the reference-quarter endpoint; do not infer expiry directly from a raw month code. Store the actual calendar. Its $25/basis-point convention is distinct from a heuristic $1 million equivalent notional. [SOFR conventions](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/articles-and-reports/understanding-sofr-futures), [SR3 rules](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/rulebook/CME/IV/400/460.pdf), [notional explanation](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/whitepapers/a-practitioners-guide-to-three-month-sofr-futures-contract-notional.html).

Version sessions in America/Chicago and UTC, including overnight trade dates, daylight saving, maintenance, holidays, early closes and halts. Freeze the exact daily decision/entry/exit clocks. Next equity session and next futures trade date need an explicit mapping. Record settlement publication separately from its valuation window. Executable ZN policy must exit/roll before the earliest applicable notice/delivery deadline with a prespecified buffer. [Trading calendar](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading-hours.html), [expiration calendar](https://www.cmegroup.com/tools-information/calendars/expiration-calendar.html).

## Timestamps, mappings and rolls

Databento definitions supply point-in-time symbol, activation/expiration and tick metadata; precision may be date-only. Retain instrument IDs, definition changes and mappings rather than identifying a position by root alone. For ordinary records, preserve exchange `ts_event`, provider `ts_recv`, sequence/flags and actual local receipt/processing. Provider receipt is not the user's receipt. Null historical local clocks remain null; replay latency is separately assumed. [Definitions](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/instrument-definitions), [timestamp conventions](https://databento.com/docs/standards-and-conventions/common-fields-enums-types).

Interval BBO differs: `ts_recv` marks interval end; `ts_event` is the last trade timestamp. Neither proves the original quote update time. Use sampled BBO for a declared context/pricing study; an execution claim needs appropriate event-level freshness, sizes and latency evidence. [BBO specification](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/bbo).

Databento continuous symbols map to actual contracts with unadjusted prices: `.c.0` ranks expiry, `.v.0` prior-day volume and `.n.0` prior-close open interest. “Previous day” still requires availability evidence at the decision; open-interest reporting may arrive later. Preserve daily resolution and source publication clocks. [Symbology documentation](https://databento.com/docs/standards-and-conventions/symbology).

Freeze an ex ante calendar roll per product before outcome inspection; select the next eligible maturity using known schedules. A separately registered lagged-liquidity variant may use only already published statistics. Never select with today's completed volume, future open interest or hindsight profitability. Compute lagged returns within raw contracts; crossing an unadjusted splice creates an artificial move. Any adjusted feature series needs a causal, versioned transformation using roll information known then; future back-adjustments must not rewrite earlier features. Labels/P&L always use raw selected contracts, explicit close/open roll fills and costs, never synthetic-series price differences.

## Features, labels and capital accounting

Initial context features: eligible lagged ES/ZN returns, realized volatility, overnight movement ending before decision, spread/size, calendar-spread slope, maturity/roll proximity and synchronized basis where supported. Debt maturity, fixed/floating mix and dated company-network exposures can justify train-only interactions. A futures price change is not automatically a yield change; ZN DV01 needs dated deliverable/CTD assumptions, and SR3 is a quarter-average rate exposure. Basis requires simultaneous underlying evidence; a closed cash index is stale during overnight futures trading.

Keep future next-session direction, dollar P&L, volatility and portfolio-plus-hedge loss in a separate label artifact with availability/censoring. Futures outcomes shared across many companies are common market observations, not independent samples. Aggregate any company signals into one capital-constrained market position through a frozen policy; prevent replicated issuer rows from multiplying apparent profits.

Conditional legs include beta-scaled ES hedges or exposure-supported Treasury/SOFR hedges, with train-only sizing and integer contracts. These bear issuer, beta, curve and basis risk. Relative-value predictions also bear risk. A strict same-underlying replicating-arbitrage claim additionally requires matching deliverables/settlement and simultaneous executable legs, financing, dividends, stock borrow/recall, fees and constraints. An issuer stock versus ES/ZN relationship cannot satisfy that identity. Equity-index carry depends on financing and expected dividends; Treasury basis adds repo and delivery considerations. [Index fair value](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/fairvalue.html), [Treasury delivery guide](https://www.cmegroup.com/content/dam/cmegroup/trading/interest-rates/files/us-treasury-futures-delivery-process.pdf).

Account for raw-contract fills, variation cashflows, initial/maintenance collateral, changing broker requirements, collateral interest/funding, commissions, spread, slippage and roll costs. Variation settles cash through clearing cycles; record broker cash timing separately. Reconcile cashflows plus residual mark without double-counting P&L. Use shared account equity and cash buffers as capital denominators, with margin shortfall/forced liquidation scenarios; initial margin alone is not invested wealth. [Performance bonds](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/brochures-and-handbooks/101-overview-cme-clearing-performance-bond-practices.html), [settlement cycles](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/brochures-and-handbooks/101-overview-settlement.html).
