The 37-row peer manifest does not establish this. Do not infer the exhibit hash from the retained 10-K/10-Q or re-download bulk history.

Record byte identity if present, or a reproducible normalized text comparison plus differences and quote-span mappings. Title/figure spot checks above are insufficient.

Find a historically captured, independently timestamped copy of that same wording version (for example, a contemporaneous archive or wire delivery receipt with preserved content), or another documented conservative upper-bound proof accepted by the canonical clock owner.
