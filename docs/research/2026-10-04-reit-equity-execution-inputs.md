# REIT wording pilot: minimum stock execution and cost inputs

**Research note, 2026-10-04.** Scope is the Post Benchmark owner's first *post-release wording → common-stock* hypothesis for the frozen four-issuer universe (AMT, AAT, AGNC, BXMT): hypothetical USD 1 million, long and short books of equal absolute notional, at most 100% combined gross, zero cash yield, development data from January 2024 onward, and a protected chronological out-of-sample period. The immediate retained-event price check concerns only the owner's two AMT 2024 events. This note identifies evidence needed for that stock-only economic test; it does not authorize an economic consumer, fit a model, open the holdout, or report P&L. The active owner protocol uses next eligible regular-session open + 60 seconds for entry, close − 60 seconds for exit, and at most 60 seconds of **raw quote-update** age. Do not substitute a 15:30 exit or interval endpoint for that rule.

**Stock boundary.** A common share is one share, subject to split/action conversion; option contract multipliers, deliverables, American exercise and assignment are **not mandatory inputs** to this stock-only arm. The retained OPRA/GLBX rows do not cure stock borrow or stock execution gaps. Conversely, a corporate filing dividend total is not automatically a per-share ex-date cash flow, and an issuer loan relationship is not stock-loan availability.

**Decision.** The retained five-symbol, two-year BBO purchase is useful for coverage and price diagnostics, but only AMT/AAT/AGNC/BXMT belong to the frozen pilot; O remains diagnostic. Issuer/filing evidence can begin an action ledger. These inputs do **not** yet support a costed long/short return series or Sharpe claim. Prioritize (1) action/identity reconciliation, (2) existing-entitlement/raw-file freshness and capacity for the two retained AMT 2024 events, (3) dated broker borrow and account terms, and (4) trade-date fee/slippage rules. Any broader Databento source idea remains unpriced and unchecked; there is no new purchase, API/account signup, annual tick reacquisition, OOS opening, or economic consumer acceptance in this note. The [qualification report](../../data/processed/reit_build/20261004-continuation/market-v2-results/qualification_report.json) itself marks `consumer_accepted=false`, `canonical_consumer_ready=false`, and `cost_evidence={}`.
