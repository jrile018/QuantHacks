# Fresh evaluation and paper readiness

## What this component can freeze

The registered v2 runner selects at most one Lattice calibration using only purged2021/2022/2023 development losses. All attempted settings remain in the ledger. Its selected arm and calibration, source/config/input hashes, eligibility rules, zero fallback, target and forecast units can be frozen as an offline numerical component. A zero selection is a valid decision to contribute no additional Lattice forecast. It is not a finding about an eligible after-cost trading strategy.

The2024/2025 reports are already inspected development diagnostics. No protected final-test data is opened. A subsequent paper test needs new, qualified observations after the freeze; future sessions cannot be completed in this run. The runner's output is not an order or a deployed strategy.

## Gates before a new evaluation

| Boundary | Required evidence | Current handling |

| Futures | Actual contract at entry/exit, financial dollars per point, explicit roll legs if needed, sessions, dated margin/settlements/costs | Contract units are not assumed dollars per point |
| Options | Authoritative deliverable/multiplier, lifecycle/assignment/settlement, synchronized underlying clocks and fees | Assumed100 and daily option marks do not supply these facts |
| Account and inference | Post-owned replay/acceptance, regular net cash-inclusive account returns and matched baseline; then the inference owner's accepted return contract | No second economic ledger, Sharpe or profit inferred from forecast error |

A Lattice feature may be used in the earlier15:30 pilot only if its input cutoff is lawful at that decision. A previous completed close can be a causal feature, but changing the holding interval creates a different target and requires new registration and evaluation. Forecast gains on the v2 basket do not transfer automatically to individual shares, futures hedges or option premiums.

## Frozen component receipt

The final study report will bind the selected setting and the exact source/config/input versions in a readiness JSON packet. Status remains `not_started_pending_qualified_fresh_inputs`; no automatic paper scheduler, capital sizing or trade instruction follows from it. The Post consumer owns economic acceptance and the existing first wording recipe. Data owners supply the missing dated evidence; the study supplies numerical diagnostics and explicit refusals.

## Grill decisions and counterexamples

- Daily/next-session remains primary;5/20 sessions cannot rescue a failed primary test.
- Futures' direct profit and hedge benefit remain separate future economic evaluations.
- Improvement must beat zero and the strongest simple registered forecast on identical outcomes. The state-only, drift-only and combined arms test whether historical drift explains the gain.
- Split-history gating checks price-fit consistency. It does not prove that a company shock is temporary or that a business link exists.
- Even a predictive improvement can lose money after spreads, fees, borrow, funding or lifecycle effects. Missing evidence stays insufficient rather than becoming a no-edge claim.
- Mean-reversion fixtures validate conditional mechanics. The500 block draws quantify exploratory frozen-loss uncertainty; they do not optimize all parameters or prove generalization.
