# Lattice: risk, volatility, options and hedge applications

Research date: 2026-10-03. Primary papers, author manuscripts, journal records and exchange/clearing documents only. This is a falsifiable implementation note, not a new backtest or trading recommendation.

## Decision in the present dataset

The existing 15 fixed equity/ES/ZN/CL/GC forecast comparisons gave **context plus Lattice the lowest error in zero cells**. The options audit has 34 attempted events but only 18 distinct events in its original primary sample, below its frozen 20-event gate. The 2024/2025 outcomes are already inspected. The equity data are daily adjusted closes for 12 fixed companies from 2018 onward; the futures sample is 2024/2025 same-contract interval BBO with only 60–61 qualified training rows in the reported experiment. No synchronized option IV/rate/dividend/deliverable history is available. These are [project observations](../2026-10-03-contextual-lattice-results.md), not conclusions from the cited papers. The approved separate 5/20 secondary options diagnostic cannot replace the failed primary gate.

The most useful Lattice application now is **risk estimation on a fixed equity book**, evaluated at equal opportunity and after rebalance costs. A graph can describe dependence or constrain a covariance estimator; graph structure alone says nothing about next-session returns or economic transmission. A second small volatility-risk study is possible with daily proxies, explicitly labeled as such. A portfolio-plus-futures hedge study is conditional on a fixed portfolio and synchronized dollar P&L. Options relative value should remain a data-contract design until executable chains exist.

## What the primary methods actually buy

| Method and primary source | Implementable output and economic role | Required comparator and critical limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Ledoit–Wolf linear covariance shrinkage](https://www.ledoit.net/Well-conditioned2004.pdf) | Estimate a positive, better-conditioned return covariance matrix; supply portfolio variance and long-only minimum-variance weights. | Compare sample covariance, diagonal/inverse-vol and 1/N. Shrinkage improves estimation under its loss criterion; it is neither a return forecast nor proof of lower realized portfolio risk here. |
| [Laloux et al. noise in stock correlation eigenvalues](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.1467) | Prespecified eigenvalue/PCA cleaning can remove unstable cross-sectional components before covariance estimation. | Compare the same optimizer with plain/shrunk covariance and a small factor/PCA covariance. At 12 names, cleaning may add complexity without enough incremental benefit. |
| [López de Prado hierarchical risk parity](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2708678) | Cluster correlation distances, quasi-diagonalize and allocate recursively; output long-only weights without covariance inversion. | Compare 1/N, inverse-vol and shrinkage minimum variance under identical constraints, gross exposure and rebalance clock. The paper's Monte Carlo result is not validation of this 12-name book. |
| [TMFG, Massara–Di Matteo–Aste](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1477623/) and [LoGo, Barfuss et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.07349) | TMFG filters a similarity matrix into a sparse planar graph; LoGo uses its local cliques/separators to estimate a sparse inverse covariance. Use the resulting risk model for conditional stress/risk allocation. | Compare Ledoit–Wolf, PCA/factor covariance and [graphical lasso](https://academic.oup.com/biostatistics/article/9/3/432/224260) with identical inputs. TMFG is a filtering algorithm; LoGo's published high-dimensional efficiency does not imply an edge at 12 names. A partial-correlation edge is not a signed customer/supplier edge or causal shock channel. |
| [Corsi HAR-RV](https://academic.oup.com/jfec/article-abstract/7/2/174/856522) | Forecast *realized* volatility from daily, weekly and monthly realized-volatility components once suitable intraday observations exist. | Compare lagged RV, EWMA, rolling historical volatility and a constant training mean. Close-to-close squared returns can support a separately named noisy daily-volatility-proxy model; they are not Corsi's intraday realized-volatility input. |
| [Diebold–Yilmaz variance-decomposition networks](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17490) | A fitted multivariate volatility model yields directional forecast-error-variance shares and connectedness statistics for risk monitoring. | Compare simple mean correlation, common-factor volatility, own HAR/EWMA and a no-network forecast. Direction in a VAR decomposition is model-dependent statistical predictability, not causal business exposure. The small current futures training sample is especially unsuitable for an unrestricted multivariate VAR. |

[DeMiguel, Garlappi and Uppal](https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhm075) make 1/N a serious portfolio benchmark: estimation error can wipe out gains from optimized weights. Use it as a comparator rather than a token control. All covariance variants must use past-only windows, a common return definition, the same missingness rule and frozen hyperparameter selection. Correlation of adjusted equity returns is dimensionless; covariance is return-squared per session. A futures covariance built from standardized point changes is in risk-unit-squared, so dollar hedge sizing additionally needs point multipliers, contract identity and an explicit risk-scale conversion.

## How each lane becomes an economic decision

### 1. Equity covariance/risk policy — first narrow implementation

**Decision/objective.** At completed session (t), estimate covariance using only eligible history through (t). Hold a fixed, long-only 12-name universe, identical maximum per-name weight and gross budget. Candidate weights: 1/N, inverse volatility, shrinkage minimum variance, HRP, and only then TMFG/LoGo or graphical lasso. Measure next-session fixed-book dollar/account P&L, realized variance/downside/tail loss, concentration, turnover and after-cost performance. Risk reduction is the claim; do not score return alpha simply because the optimizer chose different stocks. Freeze any return or tracking-error floor relative to the fixed reference before evaluation. Compare equal average gross exposure and include a cash-scaled reference so apparent protection is not merely deleveraging.

**Gate.** First require valid past-only price/identity/adjustment vintages and a common investable session grid. On exploratory 2024/2025 data, report paired risk/cost deltas as diagnostics; no fresh holdout remains there. Promote only after new chronological observations and a prespecified materially useful risk reduction versus the best simple policy, with uncertainty, opportunity cost and maximum drawdown shown. Turnover, whole-share rounding, spreads/fees, corporate actions and entry/exit availability belong in the ledger. Daily adjusted closes alone establish a statistical risk comparison, not executable fills.

**Smallest graph increment.** Export one graph-conditioned covariance/risk forecast and graph stability score beside the shrinkage baseline. Require the graph to improve the *same* frozen risk policy's paired objective; a picture, centrality shift or denoised in-sample likelihood does not pass. If shrinkage or 1/N wins, retain the graph as descriptive context only.

### 2. Volatility-risk forecast — conditional small implementation

**Decision/objective.** With current daily closes, forecast next-session squared/absolute return or book variance as a noisy proxy and test whether lagged cross-name correlation/stability adds to own lag, rolling volatility and EWMA. Use squared returns in return-squared units and absolute returns in absolute fractional-return units; never interchange them. Once timestamped intraday prices are available, separately construct RV as the sum of intraday squared log returns over a prespecified session and fit HAR daily/weekly/monthly components. A network extension can use lagged realized measures, but requires a defensible low-dimensional model and enough training observations.

**Gate.** Assess paired out-of-sample variance forecast loss/calibration and a fixed downstream sizing or risk-cap policy *against* the own-volatility baseline. Keep forecast accuracy distinct from economic utility. The observed 2024/2025 panel is development data. The 60–61-row futures fit is insufficient grounds to claim multivariate volatility connectedness or select among many graph variants. Corsi's and Diebold–Yilmaz's published applications do not establish this next-session improvement.

### 3. Futures hedge of a fixed equity book — conditional, not standalone futures alpha

**Decision/objective.** Fix the underlying equity portfolio and its rebalance policy first. Let (P_t) be its dollar P&L and (F_t) the contemporaneous *same-contract* ES or MES dollar P&L per contract; estimate a past-only minimum-variance hedge (h=\operatorname{Cov}(P,F)/\operatorname{Var}(F)), with sign convention written in the ledger. Compare no hedge, static beta/OLS hedge, volatility-scaled hedge and a graph-covariance hedge. [Ederington's original futures hedge study](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1979.tb02077.x) motivates minimum-variance hedge effectiveness; it does not show that a graph improves this book. Dollar P&L, not the current futures target's point-change/prior-point-std risk units, is the account outcome. [CME's ES specification](https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/equities/sp/e-mini-sandp500.contractSpecs.html) and [MES contract FAQ](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/faqs/frequently-asked-questions-micro-e-mini-equity-index-futures.html) set contract multipliers and tick economics; MES is an implementation size, not an independent signal. Margin requirements change with conditions [per CME](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/articles-and-reports/understanding-margin-changes).

**Gate.** Same decision/entry/exit clocks for book and hedge, BBO side/size and nonfill modeling, fees, tick/contract multiplier, roll and expiration schedule, funding/cash and initial/maintenance margin. Show residual risk, downside/tails, basis exposure, hedge drag and account return after costs at comparable beta and capital budget. A midpoint or sampled 09:36 BBO mark is a diagnostic, not a fill. The hedge value can be real even if futures direction forecasts remain unpromoted; a hedge that simply reduces equity beta needs to beat static beta or cash-scaled controls.

## Options: distinct hypothesis, currently blocked

The primary literature asks different questions. [Goyal–Saretto 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2009.01.001) sorts individual options by historical realized minus at-the-money implied volatility and reports *monthly* option returns. [Carr–Wu](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/3/1311/1581057) synthesize variance swap rates from option strips and study variance risk premia; the model-free index example is operationalized by [Cboe's VIX methodology](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/vix/VIX_Methodology.pdf), which uses SPX option bid/ask quotations and near/next expiries for a 30-calendar-day quantity. These are not one-session straddle midpoint or adjusted-stock-movement formulas. [Bollerslev–Tauchen–Zhou](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/732839) find market-return predictability at an intermediate quarterly horizon using model-free option-implied and intraday realized variance; that horizon and data construction do not transfer to next-session single-name trades. [Goyal–Saretto 2025](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/38/6/1783/8010873) show much of previously reported delta-hedged option-strategy profitability is accounted for by option factors, with no significant strategy alpha after their factor adjustment and transaction costs. This is a strong adverse benchmark for a purported Lattice option edge.

**Candidate once data exist.** Freeze exact underlying, timestamp, expiry/maturity band, strike/delta, put/call, exercise style and deliverable. Compare term-matched annualized implied **variance** with a real-time physical expected variance forecast over the *same* interval: (VRP_{t,T}=K^{Q}_{var,t,T}-\widehat{E}^{P}_{t}[RV_{t,T}]). Record the ex-post payoff separately as (K^{Q}_{var,t,T}-RV_{t,T}). ATM IV squared is a narrower proxy than model-free variance-swap fair value; label it accordingly. A graph hypothesis might condition physical variance on peer shocks or compare an option's IV residual against a dated peer surface, but it must beat own-history/HAR, sector/market IV, maturity/moneyness and ordinary option-factor baselines. Do not infer a short-vol trade from a positive spread: variance risk, jump/tail exposure, hedging rebalances, spreads and borrow can rationally price it.

**Data and ledger gate.** Need synchronized option BBO (bid, ask, size, condition, original timestamp), underlying BBO/print and option-chain identity at decision/entry/exit; rate and dividend/forward inputs known then; strike, expiration, multiplier, exercise style and historical corporate-action deliverable. [OCC's options disclosure document](https://www.theocc.com/company-information/documents-and-archives/options-disclosure-document) and [OCC adjustment records](https://www.theocc.com/clearance-and-settlement/tax-basis-reporting) establish the contract-lifecycle issue. Record side-specific execution, fees, borrow/funding, delta shares and rehedge clock, assignment/exercise, close/expiry and missing/nonfill state. Keep the 18-event primary gate and approved 5/20 secondary diagnostic separately labeled; neither supplies a valid new holdout or synchronized historical chain. The existing 34 attempts/31 descriptive mark pairs cannot verify IV, VRP or tradable P&L.

## Minimum useful integration/export contract

The Lattice producer should send Post Benchmark **one versioned, immutable risk capsule** per eligible decision, preserving the original market/unit boundaries. Suggested fields: `protocol_id`, `source_hashes`, `universe_and_identity_vintage`, `decision_ts_et`, `latest_input_ts`, `availability_status/reason`, `lookback_and_fit_cutoff`, `return_unit`, `covariance_unit`, `portfolio_policy_id`, `gross_exposure`, `estimated_covariance_or_hash`, `graph_method_and_frozen_params`, `graph_stability`, `baseline_risk`, `graph_risk`, `candidate_weights`, and `cost_assumptions`. For a futures hedge add `portfolio_dollar_exposure`, `contract_id`, `point_multiplier`, `entry/exit_quote_ts`, `BBO_side_size`, `roll/expiry`, `margin_assumption`, `hedge_contract_count`; for options add exact option symbol/deliverable, expiry, IV/variance unit and all synchronized inputs. Missing fields remain explicit missing/abstain, never filled from later outcomes. Consumer owns the central fit/adapter and paired evaluation, avoiding duplicate model selection inside the capsule.

The acceptance trace is a single decision that reconciles feature cutoff → graph/baseline forecast → selected weights/hedge → eligible side-specific quote → fill/nonfill → realized account cash flows, including zero-trade days. Preserve all candidate outputs, objective units, cost scenarios, rejected gates and hashes. The new question is incremental risk or pricing information **over the strongest simple substitute on identical eligible rows**, with the 2024/2025 research search and all prior Lattice trials disclosed. Only genuinely later forward observations can act as a fresh test.

## Priority and disqualifying assumptions

| Priority | Slice | Present readiness | Main falsification |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12-name equity covariance/risk policy with shrinkage first, graph as one challenger | Daily historical statistical comparison feasible; executable account claim waits for execution/corporate-action vintages | HRP/TMFG/LoGo fail to improve risk after turnover versus 1/N, inverse-vol and shrinkage at equal exposure. |
| 2 | Daily volatility-proxy forecast/risk cap; intraday HAR-RV as a separate future data tier | Daily proxy feasible; true RV and credible directed connectedness require more data | Own lag/EWMA/rolling volatility explains the apparent network gain. |
| 3 | Fixed-book ES/MES hedge diagnostic | Conditional on a frozen equity book, synchronized dollar marks, costs and margin; 2024/2025 already inspected | Static beta hedge or simply less equity exposure explains any risk reduction. |

Options RV–IV/VRP is outside these three implementation slices until the chain/lifecycle contract is met. No graph method creates signed economic links, pre-event expectations, complete news coverage, or option quotes that the project does not have. Those missing elements are disqualifying for causal business-network and option-profit claims, irrespective of numerical model sophistication.
