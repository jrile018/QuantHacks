| P1 interpretation | **Native boundary model assumption** | `stat-arb/libs/gm-boundaries/src/mahalanobis.cpp:117-145` and `fastmcd.cpp:597-621` calculate `D²=(x−μ̂)'Σ̂⁻¹(x−μ̂)`, then `p=Pr(χ²_k≥D²)` and `inside=D≤sqrt(qχ²_k(1−α))`. ## Interpretation of why performance was worse

The negative study is primarily a **feature usefulness** result, not evidence of a discovered sign/normalization bug. The native “sphere” is a compressed map of correlations. Being far from peers or a prior cloud can occur because volatility and common factors changed; the mapping does not establish an expected **direction** or an option premium gap. The separate Python adaptations tested those missing prediction links against zero/mean and ordinary-peer controls. On the same stock target, zero had lower error at 1/5/20 sessions (`docs/research/lattice-implementation/results-v1.md`), and context+Lattice was best in none of 15 predeclared market/hypothesis cells (`docs/research/2026-10-03-contextual-lattice-results.md`). This does not invalidate the geometry calculations; it rejects those specific forecasting interpretations on the already-inspected panel. The futures cells had only 60–61 qualified training rows, and market-data vintage/clock limits remain explicit.

## Source basis

- [scikit-learn robust covariance documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.covariance.EllipticEnvelope.html) describes the elliptical outlier model as Gaussian and distinguishes raw/corrected covariance.
- [scikit-learn Mahalanobis example](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/covariance/plot_mahalanobis_distances.html) gives the Gaussian `D²` formula and notes estimation of location/covariance in practice.
- [SciPy Gaussian KDE documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.gaussian_kde.html) specifies Scott's `n^(−1/(d+4))` bandwidth factor and warns that bandwidth affects density shape and multimodal data can be oversmoothed.

No native library compilation or real-data refit was performed for this audit lane. The numeric witnesses above follow directly from the source formulas; the parent audit owns any remote fixture execution.
