## Severity and scope corrections

- The phrase **“shrunk-covariance Mahalanobis”** in `mahalanobis.hpp:3-5` disagrees with `mahalanobis.cpp:69-95`, which inverts an ordinary covariance of MAD-standardized points. This is a confirmed naming/contract defect, but calling the naming alone P1 overstates the demonstrated operational impact. The separate calibration issue above carries the substantive P1 interpretation risk.
- The one-factor equal-book identity in `signals-and-adaptation.md` holds for the active branch `factor_variance > VARIANCE_FLOOR` (`risk.py:31-39`), which the saved 12-equity diagnostic uses. The zero-variance fallback sets beta to zero and does not obey the same identity. The report should state this condition when presenting the formula as exact.
- `native-spectral.md` is careful to distinguish the MP continuous lower edge from the zero atom: the native witness returns `lambda_minus=0` at `q=4/3`, while the continuous edge is `0.0239322566`; `rmt.cpp` uses the upper edge for clipping. This is metadata, not an active clipping error. The native OU `dt` guard is also confirmed by the witness (rejected at `dt=1`, accepted at `dt=1/252` despite the same 59-pair span), but the app's default `dt=1` and the Python adaptation do not use that faulty branch.
- The circular one-factor control is an estimation design limitation, not an algebraic bug. With the active factor branch and equal weights, the residual book sums to zero, so replacing its full covariance by a diagonal adds a nonnegative variance term. Better datewise QLIKE can arise from that mechanical uplift and does not identify an independent factor advantage.
- `audit_frozen_targets.py:24-45` correctly separates `available_basket` from `valid_model`, uses a model's own `hedge_outcome`, and compares each candidate only with zero. It filters to finite recorded forecasts and outcomes; it cannot score no-trade rows without a frozen basket. Its 20-date circular-block intervals are unadjusted, post-result and on reused 2024–2025 outcomes. The source manifest records the rescorer hash and the receipt records zero refits. These checks support the *calculation*, not untouched validation.
- Ten focused tests and one compiled native witness do not establish >80% coverage, a full native test pass, production data cleanliness, causal profitability, or any defect in the separate options pricing/execution path. Boundary, options and contextual findings remain source-static or synthetic as labeled in their reports.
