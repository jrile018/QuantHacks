# Sharpe and Monte Carlo plan review

Date: 2026-10-04. Scope: the Sharpe research brief, its three-task implementation plan, and the lattice math-audit README/build receipt. This is a focused plan review, not a new backtest or a measurement of portfolio Sharpe.

## Recommendation

Keep the Sharpe plan as a conditional future method specification, but do not treat its three deliverables as the next work for the current lattice result. The accepted Post Benchmark capsule contains descriptive forecast and risk-component rows, not an accepted after-cost portfolio equity/return ledger. The math audit reports that the frozen own-hedge MSE is worse than zero and that the daily sign mark is −0.91 bp/opportunity before costs; neither is net portfolio performance. The defensible current decision is **no demonstrated positive incremental edge / no-trade pending an eligible costed comparison**. No measured strategy Sharpe, confidence interval, or annualized Sharpe follows from these inputs.

If the requested form forces a subjective prior for the *after-cost out-of-sample portfolio Sharpe increment*, use zero as an explicitly subjective, non-measured prior. Total portfolio Sharpe remains indeterminate without the holdings, capital, calendar, risk-free series, and accepted costed ledger. This prior is not a confidence interval and does not satisfy or trigger the 95% interval requirement for measured economic Sharpe.

## Scope boundary for this handoff

The audit receipt certifies a scoped Graphify build and its extraction counts, not strategy integration or economics. The README says the OU equations are correct, but the pilot's raw-stock and frozen-own-hedge errors are worse than zero; native LW/RIE issues are unused by the Python pilot; and the −0.91 bp/opportunity mark is before costs. These facts support withholding an incremental-edge assumption, not inventing an annual Sharpe estimate. The plan should therefore remain a later inference and scenario-validation design, with current economic status explicitly unavailable and current action no-trade pending accepted costed evidence.

## Addendum: faster alpha pilot and LEAN

For speed, stop expanding the existing post-disclosure equity experiment: its primary frozen-hedge error is already worse than zero, and the daily sign mark is negative before costs. Do not spend time tuning that result into an alpha claim. If a quick pilot is still useful, preregister one narrow hypothesis, one horizon, one portfolio construction, and one untouched evaluation slice; compare it with no-trade on the same dates and capital, charge observable costs, and stop at a clear kill gate. A descriptive forecast improvement is not enough to pass that gate.

Keep the options question separate. A **pre-event option** hypothesis needs point-in-time option-chain/quote evidence available before the event, a defined entry clock, spread-aware fills, and contract expiry/deliverable/exercise accounting. A **post-disclosure equity** test starts after information release and evaluates a different signal, execution clock, and risk; the failed equity result cannot be rescued by calling it an options test, and an option mark cannot validate the equity test. LEAN can supply option subscriptions, historical quote data, and configurable fill models, but the exact data resolution and fill assumptions still need to match the proposed entry. Its official docs describe option data/history and fill-model behavior [here](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/securities/asset-classes/equity-options/requesting-data), [here](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/historical-data/asset-classes/equity-options), and [here](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/reality-modeling/trade-fills/key-concepts).

**Would LEAN improve backtesting?** Potentially, once the hypothesis and accepted portfolio contract are fixed: LEAN is an open-source algorithm engine with historical event processing, portfolio accounting, and replaceable fill/fee/slippage/margin models ([engine overview](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/key-concepts/algorithm-engine), [trade-fill models](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/reality-modeling/trade-fills/key-concepts)). It will not improve this pilot's speed-to-answer if the work is still exploratory forecast/MSE analysis; it adds algorithm, data, and fill-model setup, and local option backtests require appropriate datasets and the equity security master ([local options setup](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/lean-cli/backtesting/deployment)). Recommendation: keep the current diagnostic harness for the narrow falsification screen; consider LEAN only for a surviving, frozen, costed portfolio replay where its event and order lifecycle are useful. No migration or purchase is recommended in this review.

## Addendum: minimal post-release pilot

Steering selects the information available **after public release**. Keep the pilot to daily/next-session equity decisions with one ordinary, frozen post-release baseline and at most one frozen Lattice challenger. Keep no-trade as the zero-activity comparator. Do not retune the already negative pilot result into a winner; preserve its negative outcome as exploratory evidence. Defer geometry variants, options and futures.

**Producer prerequisites:** supply a versioned event table with stable issuer/security identity, event ID, source URL or artifact hash, event type, and both public-release and local receipt/availability timestamps in an explicit timezone. Preserve corrections and duplicate notices rather than silently replacing them. Supply point-in-time daily prices, corporate actions, exchange calendar, currency, and units; define the first executable observation after the later of public release and system receipt. Keep source provenance and missing/stale states. Current descriptive 21-stock forecast rows and five risk-component rows do not by themselves meet this event-and-execution contract.

**Consumer prerequisites:** freeze the baseline and optional challenger before the untouched evaluation slice. Join only on stable identity and event ID; enforce that every signal input was available by its decision timestamp. Emit one account-equity observation per scheduled valuation interval, including flat/no-trade intervals. Specify order/entry timing, executable price convention, exit rule, position sizing, overlapping-event aggregation, cash and corporate-action treatment, and separately accounted spread, fees, slippage, borrow/financing where applicable. Preserve rejected/unfilled opportunities, missing marks, and realized/unrealized lifecycle records. Return the costed ledger, hashes, calendar, baseline identity, exclusions, and eligibility status to the consumer that computes paired net-growth/risk comparisons and—only if eligible—Sharpe with its required interval.

**Decision states:** stop and record no-trade if the frozen challenger fails the predeclared incremental net-value or human-selected hard-risk gates; expand only if it improves the same-date costed baseline and passes every chosen risk/data gate on untouched evidence; report inconclusive when clocks, coverage, ledger, sample precision, or risk evidence cannot decide. The human must choose the numeric risk limits and any minimum practical benefit before the holdout is read. A negative or inconclusive result remains in the record; it does not authorize post-hoc tuning on that holdout.

Add Lattice only after the ordinary baseline yields an eligible, reproducible ledger and a distinct frozen challenger can be tested on identical events, timestamps, capital, and costs. Evidence for adding it is incremental after-cost portfolio growth over that baseline, with an uncertainty interval and all human-chosen hard-risk gates passed on untouched data; descriptive forecast/MSE changes are insufficient. LEAN becomes useful if a specific gap requires event-time order scheduling, fill/fee/slippage or margin models, or account-level order and corporate-action lifecycle replay that the current harness cannot represent. For a daily next-session screen whose execution can be faithfully represented in the existing costed ledger, LEAN adds setup without resolving the current missing event/receipt clocks or economic evidence.
