## Review criteria

Economic mechanism; incremental information beyond existing prices; executable after-cost outcomes; strict public-information chronology; adequate independent events and uncertainty; drawdown/capital/liquidity feasibility. Missing evidence stays missing. A narrow extraction score or a successful GPU run is not a trading result.

Research is complete when each major claim has a primary source or explicit inference/unknown label, the current implementation is distinguished from hypotheses, and actionable falsification tests are specified. The interview remains open until the user resolves its decision branches; research can be delivered independently.
