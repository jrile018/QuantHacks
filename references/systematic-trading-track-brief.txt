KEY TERMS
↺
BACKTEST

Running a strategy over historical data as if you’d traded it, bar by bar, using only what you would have known at each point.

The point is to find out where your idea breaks.

OOS
OUT-OF-SAMPLE

Data you set aside and never use to design or tune the strategy. It’s as close as you can get to testing on the future.

For this track: the most recent 20% of your history or the most recent 2 years, whichever is shorter.

S/σ
SHARPE RATIO

Average return divided by volatility, annualized. Roughly, how much return you got for the risk you took.

A Sharpe above 3 on daily data usually means a bug. Check before you celebrate.

▼%
MAX DRAWDOWN

The biggest drop from a peak in your equity curve to the low that came after it.

Ask yourself whether you’d have kept running the strategy through it.

⇄
TURNOVER

How much you trade per year, as a multiple of your capital. More trading means more costs.

Plenty of strategies that make money gross lose it once you charge for turnover.

bp
BASIS POINT

One hundredth of a percent (0.01%). Trading costs are usually quoted in bps per trade.

Pick a cost for your market and explain where the number came from.

NO LEADERBOARD
Returns alone don’t win anything. Judges would rather see a Sharpe of 0.6 you can defend than a 4 you can’t explain.

DATA PROBLEMS TO HANDLE (AND EXPLAIN IN THE NOTE)
SURVIVORSHIP

Today’s index members are the companies that survived. Use a point-in-time universe, or say the bias is there and estimate how much it matters.

CORPORATE ACTIONS

Splits and dividends make prices jump overnight. Use adjusted data and say how it was adjusted.

MISSING DATA

Gaps, halts and stale prints. Say whether you filled or dropped them, and never fill a gap with data from after it.

PITFALLS
COMMON WAYS BACKTESTS GO WRONG

Judges see these every year. Each one makes a backtest look better without making the strategy any better. Catch them yourself and you’ll do better on Economic Foundation and Performance.

TAP A CARD TO SEE HOW TO AVOID IT
0/9 READ
01
PROBLEM
P-HACKING
DATA SNOOPING
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Testing dozens of signals, lookbacks or thresholds and reporting only the best one.
02
PROBLEM
OVERFITTING
TOO MANY PARAMETERS
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Many free parameters, complex rules, or ML models tuned on a short history.
03
PROBLEM
LOOKAHEAD BIAS
USING FUTURE INFORMATION
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Using information not available at decision time: same-bar close for signal and fill, revised economic data, future index membership.
04
PROBLEM
SURVIVORSHIP BIAS
ONLY TODAY’S SURVIVORS
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Backtesting only on today’s index members or tickers that still trade.
05
PROBLEM
IGNORING COSTS
GROSS RETURNS ONLY
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Reporting gross returns, or assuming a high-turnover strategy trades for free.
06
PROBLEM
LEAKING THE TEST SET
TUNING ON THE TEST SET
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Looking at out-of-sample results, then going back and changing the strategy.
07
PROBLEM
MISLEADING SHARPE
SHARPE WITHOUT CONTEXT
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
Sharpe from too little data, overlapping returns, or a strategy short volatility that hides tail risk.
08
PROBLEM
REGIME DEPENDENCE
ONE GOOD PERIOD
WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
All the profit comes from one period, such as 2020 or 2022.

IGNORING MARKET IMPACT
